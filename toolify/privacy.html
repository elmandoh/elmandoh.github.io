Privacy Policy for Toolify

Last updated: May 2026

At Toolify, accessible from our official website and the Google Play Store, we prioritize the privacy of our users. This document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Toolify.

1. Information We Collect

Toolify provides system utilities such as app usage monitoring and cleaning. We may collect non-personal data related to app usage statistics and device specifications to provide these services.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on your interests. We do not share personal user data with these advertising partners.

3. Data Security

We do not store or transmit your personal files or sensitive data to any external servers. All processing for cleaning and monitoring is done locally on your device.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions, contact us via our developer website: elmandoh.github.io
