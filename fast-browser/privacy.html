Privacy Policy for Fast Lite Web Browser

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data Protection

Fast Lite Web Browser is built with privacy in mind. We do not track your search history, store your passwords, or monitor your online activity.

2. Permissions

The app only requests necessary permissions (like Internet access) required to provide a functional browsing experience.

3. Third-Party Ads

We use Google AdMob to support our development. AdMob may use device identifiers for ad personalization. No browsing data is shared with AdMob.
